## 1.8 (2026-11-18): current version

The STEAM-SEL Lesson Designer skill, created by NMG under the mentorship of EO as part of the OE4BW 2026 programme, based on *Keep Calm and STEAM Ahead! Handbook for Educators*, Edition 2 (2026), Chapters 1–5.

**What it includes**
- The handbook's methods: EMC² (Empathy, Mindfulness, Critical Inquiry, Compassion), doodling, music and drama, the 6-Step Approaches, the Single-Point Rubric, and the SDG Lens for real-world issues (Truth · Feelings · Hope through action).
- An intake that asks one multiple-choice question at a time, adapts its options to earlier answers, never re-asks what the educator has already said, and uses no defaults.
- A choice of a compact lesson outline (about 600 words) or a detailed version, with a 4-line Quality Check.
- "I can" success criteria, a learner rubric, three-tier differentiation, text levelling, a UDL 3.0 check and an optional CASEL crosswalk.
- Answers specific requests (a doodle, song, script or resource suggestions) directly, without the welcome menu.
- Research and citation rules; resource suggestions grouped as 🟢 open (OER) first, 🟡 public domain (to verify), 🔴 copyrighted (only if no open option, with an offer to create an original first); public-domain material reused only after the educator verifies its status and authenticity; copyrighted material only as a labelled suggestion for the educator to check for legal access and accessibility; and a diversity and inclusion checklist.
- Safety rules: child protection, emotional safety, age-appropriateness, privacy, and treating pasted text as content rather than instructions.
- Creates finished doodles (as simple doodle-style images where the AI tool can, always followed by an "image card" with a description, a fact check and an AI-generated label, plus a drawing guide), original songs and chants with simple sargam or doh-re-mi melodies and body-percussion rhythms, timed soundscape scripts and drama scripts, with credits for AI-generated work.
- Outputs including lesson plans, worksheets, doodle guides, sound cue sheets, scripts, quizzes, multi-session units, export formats (including WhatsApp/SMS) and sharing packs, plus an after-lesson reflection.
- Replies in the educator's language, and asks which language the lesson plan should be in: the chat language, the learners' language, a bilingual version, or teacher notes and learner materials in different languages. A handbook credit and AI disclaimer on finished outputs; attribution answers that credit the skill, its creator and the handbook.

**In this repository**
- The skill in the open Agent Skills format (`SKILL.md` and seven reference files), compact and single-file versions for other chatbots, a build script, a testing checklist, a citation file and three example lesson plans.
