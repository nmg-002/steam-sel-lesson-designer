# Examples

Sample outputs showing what the STEAM-SEL Lesson Designer creates. All three are **compact outlines** (about one page each), written following the skill. Real outputs vary with the AI tool you use and the answers you give, so treat these as illustrations, and review and adapt any plan before teaching.

| Example | Learners | Art form | Highlights |
|---|---|---|---|
| [1. The Brave Little Seed: germination](01-germination-doodle-grade-5.md) | Grade 5, rural school, no internet | Doodling | Low-vision adaptations, tactile seeds, a misconception check |
| [2. The Living Market: supply and demand](02-supply-and-demand-drama-university.md) | First-year university, lecture hall | Drama | Role-play mapped onto economic curves, roles for students who don't want to act |
| [3. Monsoon Memories: changing rains (SDG 13)](03-monsoon-memories-sdg-13-grades-6-8.md) | Grades 6–8, three sessions | Doodle, soundscape and tableaux | The SDG Lens, elders' interviews, a hopeful community action |
