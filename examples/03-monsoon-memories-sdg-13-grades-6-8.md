# Monsoon Memories: Our Changing Rains
Subjects: Geography, Science, Languages | Grades 6–8 | 3 × 40 min + homework | SDGs: **13 Climate Action**, with 6 Clean Water and 2 Zero Hunger | EMC²: all four

**The SDG Lens**
1. **Issue:** monsoon rains are becoming less predictable in many places, with longer dry spells and heavier downpours.
2. **Local story:** what elders remember about the rains, the fields and the rivers.
3. **SDGs:** 13 at the heart; 6 (water) and 2 (food) connected.
4. **Art forms:** doodle → soundscape → tableaux.
5. **EMC²:** questions below.
6. **Action:** one local adaptation idea, shared with the school.

**Success criteria**
- I can compare past and present monsoon patterns using an elder's memories and data. *(geography and science)*
- I can show the change through a doodle, a soundscape and a tableau. *(art forms)*
- I can suggest one hopeful action my community could take. *(SEL)*

| Session | Activity | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rain recording with eyes closed; "What does the monsoon mean to you?"; how a warming climate affects rainfall; prepare the interview | 40 |
| Homework | Interview an elder: when did the rains come, what changed, which songs or sayings about rain do they remember? | — |
| 2 | **Doodle:** fold a page into "Then" and "Now", with faces on the clouds, river and fields · **Soundscape:** groups use bottles, seeds and paper for "Monsoon Then" (steady rain, frogs) and "Monsoon Now" (long silence, sudden downpour) | 20 + 20 |
| 3 | **Tableaux:** Then · Now · 2050: The Future We Choose, with one-line thoughts when tapped · **Action:** each group turns one adaptation idea (rainwater harvesting, reviving a village pond, planting trees) into a poster or a proposal to the school | 25 + 15 |

- **Tiers:** *Support:* a three-question interview and a partly drawn page. *Core:* as above. *Stretch:* compare the memories with local rainfall records from the India Meteorological Department (please verify the figures before teaching).
- **Quick check (session 2):** "Point to one change between your Then and Now."

| Pillar | Debrief question |
|---|---|
| Empathy | How did the changing rains feel for the farmers and fishing families your elder described? |
| Mindfulness | Listen to both soundscapes again. What do you notice in the silence? |
| Critical Inquiry | Are memories always reliable? What does the data show, and what else might explain the changes? |
| Compassion | What could we do so that everyone in our community copes better with the rains? |

**Handle with care:** some families were affected by last year's late rains. Learners choose whether to share their own experiences; the observer and sound-effects roles are always available, and the unit closes on the hopeful 2050 tableau.

**Home connection:** share the poster with the family member who was interviewed.

✨ Quality Check
✔ Aligned · fits 3 × 40 min · all four EMC² pillars · criteria + 3 tiers · local, honest, hopeful
♿ UDL & inclusion: visual, sound and movement routes; any home language for interviews; opt-out roles
🌟 Glows: elders' voices make climate change local; ends in a real action
🌱 Grows: agree in advance who receives the proposals so learners see a response
