# The Brave Little Seed: Germination × Doodling × SEL
Subject: Science | Concept: Germination | Grade 5 | 40 min | Groups of 4 | EMC²: Empathy · Mindfulness · Critical Inquiry

**1. Objectives and success criteria**
Learners will be able to show the stages of germination and the conditions a seed needs, through a doodle story.
- I can show the stages of germination in order. *(science)*
- I can use faces and speech bubbles to show how the seed "feels" at each stage. *(art form)*
- I can explain why growing takes patience, for seeds and for people. *(SEL)*

**Materials:** paper, pencils, thick black markers, soaked seeds (moong or chana) in a cup. **No-tech:** everything is paper-based.

**2. Introduction (5 + 8 min)**
- *Hook (5):* draw a sleepy seed on the board with a speech bubble: "Do I have to wake up?" Ask: "What would help this seed wake up?"
- *Concept (8):* pass round the soaked seeds. A seed needs **water**, **warmth** and **air (oxygen)** to germinate. The root comes out first, then the shoot. Sunlight matters once the first leaves appear, for making food.

**3. Activity (5 + 14 min)**
- *Modelling (5):* draw a seed in three quick shapes, add a face, then a speech bubble. Neat drawing isn't the goal; a messy doodle that shows the idea is perfect.
- *Doodle story (14), one strip per group:*
  1. Doodle water, warmth and air as characters talking to the seed. What does each one say?
  2. Show the seed's feelings at each stage: sleepy, pushing, triumphant.
  3. Is the seed a warrior breaking its shell, or a dancer reaching up? Choose a metaphor.
- **Tiers:** *Support:* a strip with the stages already outlined; add faces and words. *Core:* the prompts above. *Stretch:* add what happens if one condition is missing.
- **Low-vision learners:** thick markers on large paper, a real seed to hold, and the "storyteller" role, describing the strip aloud.
- **Quick check:** "Point to the stage where the root appears."

**4. SEL integration**
| Pillar | How | Debrief question |
|---|---|---|
| Empathy | The seed's struggle to push through the soil | How might the seed feel just before it breaks out? |
| Mindfulness | Close, slow observation of a real seed | What did you notice that you'd never noticed before? |
| Critical Inquiry | Questioning the metaphor | Do seeds really "try"? Where does our story stop being science? |

**5. Close (8 min)**
- Gallery walk: each group shows its strip and names one stage.
- Two stars and a wish, using the "I can" statements.
- **Home connection:** soak three seeds at home and doodle what changes each day.

**6. Share:** display the strips as a class "growing wall".

✔ Aligned · fits 40 min · EMC² x3 · success criteria + 3 tiers · age-appropriate
